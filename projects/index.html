Selected Applied Science Projects

A selection of work across production-scale matching, spatial decision systems, causal experimentation, and evaluation of LLM-generated insights. These are high-level descriptions; diagrams are conceptual and do not disclose internal implementation details.

01 · Production ML & Data Engineering

Global Merchant Entity Resolution

Architected a merchant matching system processing millions of restaurant records weekly to discover missing merchants and prevent duplicate sales leads.

Restaurant data → Geographic blocking → String similarity (Levenshtein / Jaccard) + embedding similarity → Matched entities → Human-labeled evaluation

~99% precision · ~98% recall

End-to-end quality measured using human-labeled samples. Related production competition data pipelines use Apache Spark; orchestration experience includes Airflow.

02 · Geospatial Decision Science

Geospatial Market Expansion

Developed an H3-based framework combining population and merchant density with market-specific quantile classifications to identify underserved areas and guide expansion investment.

H3 cells → Population and merchant density → Market-specific quantiles → Priority areas → Local validation

Market-level investment prioritization

Validated with local teams; market-share trends were monitored rather than claimed as causal effects.

03 · Experimentation & Causal Inference

Refund Policy Experimentation

Analyzed customer support text to identify policy inefficiencies and designed user-level randomized experiments to evaluate partial refunds.

Support text analysis → Hypothesis → Random assignment → Refund cost and customer outcome measurement

Statistically significant cost reduction

Monitored satisfaction, defect rates, and 28-day customer behavior; no detected decline is not proof of equivalence.

04 · Applied LLM Research

LLM Insight Recommendation

Independent MSc research into ranking LLM-generated analytical insights by importance, interestingness, and actionability, with novelty and redundancy assessment.

Generate insights → Review and score → Assess novelty → Rank → Evaluate against reference insights

Improved early retrieval in ~80% of evaluated FiveThirtyEight cases

Evaluation used curated FiveThirtyEight datasets and multi-LLM adjudication; results varied across benchmarks.
