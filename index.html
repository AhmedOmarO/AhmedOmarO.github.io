About me

I’m a data scientist who loves turning messy data into meaningful decisions. Over the past decade, I’ve built pipelines, models, and stories that help people and products move smarter from early dashboards to machine learning systems that run at scale.

I believe great data work isn’t just about accuracy; it’s about clarity. I care about making insights approachable, models explainable, and systems scalable. I enjoy that sweet spot where business meets algorithms, where human intuition still guides the math.

Currently, I’m pursuing my Master’s in Applied Data Science at Utrecht University.
