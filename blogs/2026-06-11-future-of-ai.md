## The end of writing 

This is a relatively different blog where I write some opinions rather than explaining some technical concepts as I understand it. However this is intertwined with the tech industry as it concerns with the future of our civilization in the era of AI. 


Let me dive straight to it.

Reading and writing was our ancestor way of automating knowledge transfare. If me and you were sitting in the same room, probably we will do 1:1 chat, no reading or writing is involved, only if you want to remember something I told you, you might write it down. One other reason we write is to transfare our ideas without being physically exist in the place. for example if I wanted to communicate the ideas in this blog, I would write it and publish it on the internet so you can read throguth them without me having to meet everyone on 1:1 basis. 


So in short writing serves the same purpose of money. 
1) Medium of exchange of ideas
2) Store of ideas so you can get back to them later
